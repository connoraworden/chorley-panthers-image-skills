# Chorley Panthers brand

## Club

- Amateur rugby league, 13-a-side, with interchanges, not substitutes
- Teams: first team and A team
- The usual kick-off is 14:30 on Saturday, with some midweek evening games at 19:00

## Kit

- Black shirt with white chevron hoops across the chest and shoulders, plus thin white pinstripes below the chevrons
- O'Neills logo on the right chest as worn, Panthers crest on the left chest as worn
- Main sponsor across the stomach: FI Real Estate Management

## Crest

- A black panther head facing forwards, with a red mouth, inside a black shield
- "CHORLEY" curved across the top, "Panthers" in script across the bottom
- Always use the real crest file. Never redraw it

## Sponsor

- FI Real Estate Management: a hexagon mark in teal, yellow and grey, beside the "FI" wordmark
- Always use the real logo file

## Graphic style

- Black background, with match photos in black and white and heavily darkened
- White text only
- Colour comes only from the featured player photo and the logos
- Headline type: Anton, slanted to italic
- Name lists, opponent line and small labels: Antonio, with wide letter spacing on names
- Featured name: Brittany Signature by Creatype Studio. It is a paid font and must never be committed to this repo. If you have a licence, save it as `assets/fonts/BrittanySignature.ttf` in your installed copy of the skill and the script uses it. Otherwise it falls back to the bundled Hurricane
- Thin white rules frame the top and bottom
- Portrait 4:5 for Instagram, 1080 x 1350 or larger

## Logo files

- `assets/club-crest.png` is rendered from the club's SVG badge at chorley-panthers.co.uk
- `assets/sponsor.png` is a reversed version for dark backgrounds, made from a small public copy of the FI logo. Swap in the sponsor's official reversed logo when you have it
